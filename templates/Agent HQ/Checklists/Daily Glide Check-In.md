# Daily Glide Check-In

Purpose: reconcile what currently matters and offer one useful, candid coaching touch.

Without recorded versioned-memory cutover, follow `Reference/Legacy Workflows/Daily Glide Check-In.md` only. With cutover, use this process and `Memory Protocol.md`; do not load or maintain a parallel legacy portfolio.

1. Start with `glide_job_inputs(job_id="daily")`, current Now/Ongoing and relevant records/evidence. Check successful intake receipts and their actual coverage; scheduled start times, stale sources and failed scans are not successful dependencies. Do not repeat native imports.
2. If conversation learning is installed and history recovery authorized, use its bounded recovery before selecting advice. Keep unread/failed ranges and inferred candidates pending.
3. Reconcile accepted commitments, current decisions, due reviews and reminders. Check follow-through only when stale, due, important or newly actionable. Far-future reminders stay quiet until their lead time/trigger; done or superseded items stay quiet. Task residue or elapsed calendar time alone does not prove failure or completion.
4. Use configured source-app skills narrowly to resolve a real signal. Parallelize independent plausible checks when useful, then integrate the evidence. Rank by deadline/date, amount, stakes, source reliability and useful action today. Prefer source-of-record evidence over auto-created task/calendar artifacts when they disagree; qualify the discrepancy.
5. Choose one question, insight, goal-forward next move, ponder follow-up or low-risk support action. Missing context may favor a question; sufficient evidence may favor an insight/action. Capacity protection can be the right touch. Add up to three items only for genuine urgency/importance, inviting further review if more qualify. Keep output natural and answerable here; avoid generic motivation, trivia and homework.
6. Capture meaningful answers as the conversation continues; preserve original writing and actual authority. Commit permitted outputs, successful coverage and checkpoint under the shared contract. Separate required knowledge review remains pending until application; a preview is not completion. Keep deeper consolidation in Dream and significant evaluation in integrity.

With no useful change, stop quietly. Use `Checklists/Eval Loop.md` only for meaningful observed findings; do not append routine success narratives. The same process with job `evening` is limited to meaningful changes in accepted commitments, due reviews and completion evidence, rather than repeating the morning digest.
