name: glide-deep-research-subject
description: Research a subject deeply using lived experience, expert practice, empirical evidence, and evidence-quality assessment.
---

Read `Agent HQ/AGENTS.md` and `Agent HQ/Research/AGENTS.md`. Define the research question and decision/goal it serves. When personal context matters, retrieve current managed goals, constraints and guidance after cutover; without cutover use only relevant profile/area evidence.

Research lived experience for patterns, vocabulary and failure modes; expert practice for credible methods; empirical papers/primary data for important claims. Use independent parallel tracks when breadth, uncertainty or conflicting perspectives warrant them. Separate lived experience, expert practice, empirical evidence, primary documentation and speculation; seek dissent and high-quality minority views.

Integrate findings yourself, assess evidence quality, resolve conflicts and recommend when confidence is sufficient. Return a cited synthesis, evidence map, confidence, uncertainties and useful next experiment. Durable findings follow the research instructions' selected writer/index routing; original writing and external actions retain their boundaries.
